FUADSI Admission Notice — 2026/2027 Academic Session

Issued by the Office of the Registrar, FUADSI, Iragbiji, Osun State

The management of the Federal University of Agriculture and Development Studies, Iragbiji (FUADSI), Osun State, hereby invites applications from suitably qualified candidates for admission into its undergraduate degree programmes for the 2026/2027 academic session.

Screening Exercise: All candidates who chose FUADSI as their first choice institution in the 2026 UTME and scored a minimum of 150 in the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) examination are invited to participate in the Post-UTME/DE screening exercise.

Requirements for Eligibility:

  • Minimum JAMB score of 150 (candidates below this threshold are not eligible).
  • Minimum of five (5) O'Level credit passes including English Language and Mathematics, obtained at not more than two sittings (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB).
  • Each programme may have specific subject requirements — consult the departmental requirements for your chosen course.
  • Direct Entry (DE) candidates must possess relevant ND/HND/A-Level qualifications as stipulated.

Screening Score Computation: The composite score is derived from JAMB score (60%) and O'Level performance (40%), giving a total score out of 100.

Mode of Screening: Computer-Based Test (CBT) and O'Level credentials verification. Candidates are advised to upload valid O'Level result(s) during registration.

Important: All candidates are advised to regularly visit the FUADSI official portal and their registered email addresses for updates on screening dates, venues, and admission list releases. FUADSI does not engage any agent for admission purposes — beware of fraudsters.

About the University

Federal University of Agriculture & Development Studies

FUADSI was established to drive agricultural research, innovation, and rural development across Nigeria. Located in the historic town of Iragbiji, Osun State, the university combines academic excellence with a strong focus on applied sciences.

🌾
Agricultural Excellence
Specialising in food security, rural development, and agricultural innovation for Nigeria's growing population.
🔬
Research-Driven Learning
Emphasis on practical, research-led education across natural sciences, social sciences, and technology.
🏛️
Federal Institution
Fully accredited federal university under the National Universities Commission (NUC) with nationwide recognition.
