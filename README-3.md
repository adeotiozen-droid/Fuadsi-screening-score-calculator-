## How to Use

1. Open the portal
2. Enter your **JAMB score**
3. Select your **O'Level grades** for 5 subjects
4. Click **Calculate My Score**
5. View your composite score and eligibility status

---

## Scoring Formula

| Component | Weight | Formula |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| JAMB | 60% | (Score ÷ 400) × 60 |
| O'Level (5 subjects) | 40% | (Total Points ÷ 40) × 40 |
| **Total** | **100%** | JAMB % + O'Level % |

**Minimum JAMB score:** 150
